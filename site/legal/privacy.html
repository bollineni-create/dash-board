Privacy Policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

This policy covers bollineni.co and every app published on it or on a bollineni.co subdomain (together, the "apps"). The apps are run by Bollineni Consulting LLC ("we", "us"). We collect as little as we can, use it only to run the apps, and never sell it.

What we collect

  • Account details. If you sign in with Google or an email link, we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We do not receive your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.
  • Payment details. Payments are handled by Stripe. We see what you bought, when, and your billing email. Your card number goes to Stripe, never to us.
  • What you put into an app. Text, settings and files you enter. Many apps keep this only in your browser on your device. Apps with accounts store it in our database so it follows you between devices.
  • Usage and technical data. Page views, device and browser type, and approximate country, collected through Vercel Analytics without advertising cookies. Our host keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security and troubleshooting.

How we use it

  • To sign you in, run the features you ask for, and give you access to what you paid for.
  • To send emails you need: sign-in links, receipts, and messages about your account. We send marketing email only if you opt in, and every one has an unsubscribe link.
  • To keep the apps secure, fix problems, and understand which features people use.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Services that process data for us

We share only what each service needs to do its job:

  • Vercel: hosting and analytics
  • Neon: database for apps with accounts
  • Google: sign-in
  • Stripe: payments
  • Resend: email delivery
  • AI and data providers, such as Anthropic: some apps send the text you submit to an AI model or a market-data service to produce a result. That content is used to answer your request, not to build profiles of you.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or ourselves.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google we request only your basic profile (name, email, profile picture) to create and identify your account. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We do not transfer it to others except as needed to run the apps, and we never use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep account data while your account is open. When you delete your account, or ask us to, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except records we must keep by law, such as payment records for tax purposes. Data kept only in your browser stays on your device until you clear it.

Your choices and rights

  • Ask for a copy of your data, correct it, or have it deleted.
  • Cancel a subscription anytime from the app's billing page.
  • Remove an app's access from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Email us with any request and we will reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live, laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act or the GDPR may give you additional rights, and we will honor them.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, secrets are stored on the server and never sent to your browser, and access to production systems is limited. No system is perfectly secure, so we will tell you promptly if a breach affects your information.

Children

The apps are not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. For significant changes we will also tell signed-in users by email or in the app.
